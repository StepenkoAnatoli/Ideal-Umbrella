## Machine notes style
Notes in notes/ and plans/: one 'key: item; item' line per topic, no markdown. Whole English words, no invented abbreviations. Drop articles, filler and recaps. Copy every number, date, version, identifier, path, name, negation, hedge and aspect verb verbatim. Update with Edit, not Write; grep instead of re-reading. English only; render Hebrew or prose on request, into a sibling .he.txt. Save a learning in notes/learnings.md: a choice and why under decided; an approach dropped and why under tried and ruled out; a bug's cause found after failed attempts, or the user correcting how something is done here, under watch out.
